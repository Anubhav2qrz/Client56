Biography & Vision

Bridging Investigative Media & Grassroots Sustainability

From University Rank Holder to Frontline Public Catalyst

My journey is defined by a deep conviction that communications must serve the public good. Holding an M.Sc in Electronic Media from Anna University (where I graduated as a University Rank Holder) along with an MBA from Loyola College, Chennai and a foundational B.Sc in Microbiology, I bring a unique analytical and scientific lens to broadcast journalism and policy advocacy.

During my tenure with the Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC), I served across 14 divisions in Zone 1 (Tiruvottiyur), resolving over 150 municipal complaints, steering ground surveys for Anganwadi child health centers, and spearheading emergency community relief during Cyclone Michaung.

On screen, I have defended public viewpoints across iconic platforms including Vijay TV's Neeya Naana (where I was honored with the Best Speaker Award), Kalaignar TV's Vaa Tamizha Vaa, Sun News, and Zee Tamil, alongside conducting field interviews and community radio broadcasting at Anand Radio 89.6 FM.
